Modeling a Real Retinal Ganglion Cell

Three different models, one real neuron: a classical linear filter (LNP), a spatiotemporal CNN, and a simulated analog circuit — each predicting the same cell's spiking from the same flickering-checkerboard movie.

Dataset: 27 real primate retinal ganglion cells, recorded while watching 20 minutes of 10×10 binary spatiotemporal white noise (Chichilnisky & Pillow, Nature 2008). Every cell's receptive field below was computed directly from spike-triggered averaging over the real spike trains — nothing here is simulated data.
